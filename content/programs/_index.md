Six projects at very different stages. The labels describe maturity, not
release state: **stable** means it does its job and isn't expected to change
shape, **beta** means it's published and usable but the public contracts aren't
frozen yet, **alpha** means it runs and does real work with known gaps, and
**pre-alpha** means the interesting part isn't working yet.
